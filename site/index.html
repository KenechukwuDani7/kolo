What Kolo does

Everything a group does with money.

Collect dues

Personal pay links, automatic ticks and a live progress card. Late payers still reach the pot.

Vote to spend

Money leaves the group pot only when the majority says yes, including a group admin, with a receipt for all.

Remind and track

See who has paid, and nudge the rest without being the bad guy.

Your Kolo balance and your wallet

Two places for your money.

Your Kolo balance is for paying in the group. Your wallet is yours alone. /cashout moves money from one to the other.

Money you’ve put into Kolo, held by Roqqu. Fill it with /topup, or when someone sends you money, pays your request or pays back a split.
